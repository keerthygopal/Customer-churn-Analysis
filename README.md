Customer Churn Analysis

Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn using Microsoft Excel to identify customer segments with high churn rates and develop data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

Business Objective
The objective is to understand churn patterns across contract types, internet services, and payment methods, and identify high-risk customer groups for targeted retention strategies.

Dataset Overview
- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Retained Customers: 5,174
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.54%
- Retention Rate: 73.46%

Tools and Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Data analysis and data validation
- PivotTables and percentage-of-row-total analysis
- Charts and dashboard visualization
- KPI creation and business insight generation

Key Findings

1. Contract Type: Month-to-month customers had the highest churn rate at 42.71%.

2. Internet Service: Fiber optic customers had the highest churn rate at 41.89%.

3. High-Risk Customer Group: Customers with both fiber optic internet service and month-to-month contracts had a churn rate of 54.61%.

4. Payment Method Within the High-Risk Group: Among fiber optic customers with month-to-month contracts, electronic check users had the highest churn rate at 60.37%.

 Recommendations

- Encourage month-to-month customers to consider longer-term contracts through suitable loyalty benefits or discounts.
- Collect feedback from fiber optic customers and investigate possible service quality, pricing, or support concerns.
- Explain the convenience and benefits of automatic payment options to electronic check users.
- Test targeted retention offers for fiber optic customers with month-to-month contracts, particularly electronic check users.
- Monitor churn rates regularly to evaluate the effectiveness of retention initiatives.

Project Files
- Customer_Churn_Analysis.xlsx — Excel workbook containing the raw dataset, analysis tables, PivotTables, charts, and dashboard.

Conclusion
The analysis identifies customer segments associated with higher churn, especially month-to-month contract customers, fiber optic users, and electronic check users within the high-risk group. These insights can guide further investigation and targeted customer retention initiatives.

Note: These findings show associations in the dataset and do not establish the causes of customer churn.
